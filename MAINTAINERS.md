# Maintainers

Maintainers steward the project's vision ([docs/00_Project_Vision.md](docs/00_Project_Vision.md)),
review contributions, and are the enforcement contacts for the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Governance model: **BDFL-lite**, bound publicly by the vision principles and non-goals
([docs/15_Contribution_Guide.md](docs/15_Contribution_Guide.md) §7).

| Handle | Role | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| [@sosakib](https://github.com/sosakib) | Founding maintainer | Architecture, all areas |

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contributions plus active, constructive review participation. Invitations
are extended by existing maintainers.

## Succession

If the founding maintainer is unreachable for **> 90 days**, continuity passes to the next
maintainer listed here. Keeping this file current is itself a maintainer responsibility.
